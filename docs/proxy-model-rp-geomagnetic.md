## 3. Assumptions

1. Primary carrier is quasi-static geomagnetic-scale \(\mathbf{B}\) (not \(\rho_\mathrm{DE}\)).  
2. Transduction is non-equilibrium RP yield physics (\(\mu_B B/kT\sim10^{-7}\) is not a veto).  
3. Cryptochrome-class (or stated analogue) RP with anisotropic hyperfine — *or* in-vitro realization.  
4. Coherence/lifetime long enough for Larmor/hyperfine to compete with recombination.  
5. SNR about \(\delta\Phi\) vs noise, not \(\mu_B B\) vs \(kT\).  
6. **ELF tilt budget closed as marginal:** \(b_\perp=0.1\,\mu\mathrm{T}\) on \(50\,\mu\mathrm{T}\) \(\Rightarrow\) tilt \(\approx0.115^\circ\), \(\delta\lvert B\rvert/\lvert B\rvert\approx2\,\mathrm{ppm}\); \(T_{50\mathrm{Hz}}/ \tau_\mathrm{RP}\sim2\times10^{4}\) \(\Rightarrow\) quasi-static over an RP event. No longer listed as an open prerequisite for scaffolding.  
7. No ATP-scale metabolic-drive claim.
