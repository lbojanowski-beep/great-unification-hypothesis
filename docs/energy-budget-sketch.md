# Energy-Budget Sketch Table — GUH Option A

**Document type:** Order-of-magnitude comparison sketch (not a final model)  
**Program context:** Great Unification Hypothesis (GUH) v1.2 — **Option A** (Dark Energy dropped as working brand / proxy)  
**Purpose:** Place candidate weak environmental / background field energy scales next to thermal noise (`kT`) and known biological / ambient backgrounds *before* any claim of biological sensitivity.  
**Status:** Example data / citation-backed sketch for program hygiene. Numbers are SI + eV where helpful; many entries are intentionally labeled as order-of-magnitude.

---

## 1. What this table is / is not

### Is
- A **sanity filter** for Option A candidate proxies: compare interaction scales to `kT`, to ATP chemistry, and to known ambient EM backgrounds.
- A place to park **Dark Energy (DE) density** as a *comparison row only* — showing why naive DE→biology coupling is energetically implausible — **not** as the working proxy.
- A template for filling in a **chosen environmental / vacuum / background proxy** with explicit coupling, per-molecule/cell energy, and SNR vs known scales.

### Is not
- A dynamical model of any transducer (radical pairs, stochastic resonance, ion channels, etc.).
- Evidence that any weak field *does* affect biology.
- A claim that energy-density comparisons alone decide detectability (spin chemistry and non-equilibrium amplifiers can evade naive `≪ kT` vetoes — see §5).
- Final cosmology or biophysics; several DE and ELF figures are rounded literature consensus values.

**Framing note — thermal energy density vs `kT` per degree of freedom:**  
Classical equipartition assigns \(\tfrac12 kT\) per quadratic degree of freedom. Macroscopic “thermal energy density” of a condensed phase is huge (\(\sim n\cdot kT\) with \(n\sim 10^{28}\)–\(10^{29}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}\) for condensed matter) and is **not** the right veto scale for a single molecular sensor. The relevant comparison for a proposed molecular interaction is almost always **interaction energy per relevant degree of freedom / molecule / coherent subsystem** vs \(kT\) (and vs known biological energy currencies such as ATP), unless a named non-equilibrium amplifier is specified.

## 4. How to read this (decision rule for Option A)

1. **Compute the proposed interaction energy** in a stated framing: per molecule, per coherent spin system, per cell, or per second (power × time), with an explicit coupling/transducer hypothesis.
2. **Compare to \(kT\)** (~0.026 eV at 300 K) **and** to known biological currencies (ATP ~20 \(kT\) per hydrolysis under physiological conditions).
3. **Compare to known backgrounds:** geomagnetic \(\mu_B B\), residential ELF \(B\)/\(E\), and (if optical) photon energies.
4. **Veto (dead on arrival)** if the proposed interaction is \(\ll kT\) **and** \(\ll\) known biological pathway energies **and** no **named amplifier** is provided (examples of named amplifiers: radical-pair spin chemistry / recombination yields; stochastic resonance with a specified threshold system; non-equilibrium pumped cycles; coherent averaging with quantified \(N\) and coherence time).
5. **DE row is diagnostic only:** \(\rho_\mathrm{DE}\times V_\mathrm{cell}\ll kT\) explains why Option A dropped DE as working brand. Do not treat \(\rho_\mathrm{DE}\) as a biological drive term without a new, explicit coupling that survives steps 1–4.
6. **Passing the energy-budget sketch is necessary, not sufficient.** Mechanism, SNR, noise spectra, and null controls remain separate requirements.

## 5. Caveat — radical-pair magnetoreception (do not misuse \(\mu_B B\) vs \(kT\))

At Earth-strength fields (\(B\sim50\,\mu\mathrm{T}\)),

\[
\frac{\mu_B B}{kT} \sim 10^{-7}.
\]

A naive equilibrium Curie / Boltzmann polarization argument would conclude that biology cannot sense the geomagnetic field. That argument is **the wrong physical picture** for the radical-pair (RP) hypothesis:

- RP magnetoreception is a **spin-chemistry / kinetic** effect: magnetic fields (external + hyperfine) modulate **singlet–triplet interconversion** and thereby **recombination product yields**, not equilibrium spin alignment.
- The Zeeman interaction sets a Larmor timescale (\(\sim1.4\,\mathrm{MHz}\) at 50 µT), competitive with hyperfine-driven dynamics and radical lifetimes — a **rate** comparison, not an energy-vs-\(kT\) comparison.
- Therefore: **\(\mu_B B \ll kT\) does not by itself kill a spin-chemistry claim**; it *does* kill claims that rely on equilibrium magnetic polarization or on dumping \(\mu_B B\) as free energy into a thermal bath degree of freedom.

**Implication for GUH Option A:** if a candidate proxy leans on RP-like physics, the energy-budget table must be supplemented with coherence time, hyperfine scales, recombination branching, and yield sensitivity — not a Zeeman-vs-\(kT\) ratio alone. If it does *not* lean on such a mechanism, the naive \(\ll kT\) veto stands.

Primary review: Hore & Mouritsen, *Annu. Rev. Biophys.* 45, 299–344 (2016), DOI: [10.1146/annurev-biophys-032116-094545](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-biophys-032116-094545).
