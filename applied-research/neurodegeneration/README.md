## Purpose

This brief lists **questions that would need answering** before any neurodegeneration-related research program could honestly claim a link to GUH. It is not a proposal to treat Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s, or ALS.

If GUH remains without a specified field variable, coupling, and decisive null, **this brief should stay a placeholder**.

---

## Why this topic is high-risk

Disease names invite overinterpretation. Publishing them next to a speculative cosmological hypothesis creates an ethics and credibility hazard even with disclaimers. Prefer physics-first and methods-first work before any disease framing.

---

## Open questions (not hypotheses)

1. What *concrete environmental or laboratory field proxy* is under test (geomagnetic, ELF, radical-pair yield, vacuum-noise proxy, something else) — and why is it *not* labeled “dark energy” unless a coupling model survives fifth-force constraints?
2. What biological order parameter is predicted to shift (e.g. a specific cellular assay), and by what effect size relative to `kT` and known noise?
3. What pre-registered null result would end the line of inquiry rather than prompt a redefinition of “complexity”?
4. What existing biophysical literature already covers the same ground better (magnetoreception, stochastic resonance, standard neurodegeneration pathophysiology)?
