## Abstract

The Great Unification Hypothesis (GUH) is a **working hypothesis**: that complex biological systems might be sensitive to **weak, incompletely identified environmental or background fields**, and that any such influence must be **specified, energy-budgeted, and falsifiable** before it is scientific.

This document does **not** claim the discovery of new fundamental forces, particles, or cosmological objects.  
It establishes a **research-oriented hypothesis** intended to guide interdisciplinary inquiry at the intersection of weak-field biophysics, complex systems, and human–AI collaborative research.

---

## 1. Scope & Intent

GUH is formulated as a **working hypothesis**, not as a finalized theory.

Its purpose is to:
- explore conceptual bridges between **weak-field biophysics / environmental fields** and emergent complexity,
- provide a falsifiable research narrative,
- stimulate interdisciplinary discussion and experimentation,
- remain fully compatible with existing scientific uncertainty.

GUH does **not**:
- assert empirical proof,
- redefine established physical constants,
- replace standard cosmological models,
- propose medical, therapeutic, or engineering applications.

Cosmological dark energy may remain an open problem in fundamental physics **without** being assumed as the biological coupling partner for this program.

| Ether | Dark Energy / Vacuum Energy / Background Scalar Field | Unspecified weak environmental / background field (**pending identification**) |

**Working language:** prefer **“candidate environmental / background field (to be identified)”** or **“weak environmental field (unspecified)”**. Do **not** use Dark Energy as the default brand for GUH.

### 2.4 Clarification (Option B reserved)

A literal dark-energy / quintessence / light-scalar coupling remains a **future Option B** only if it survives fifth-force, equivalence-principle, and screening constraints. It is **not** the working brand of this document.

No claim is made regarding:
- the precise nature of any candidate field,
- its origin,
- its direct interaction mechanisms with matter or life,

until a named proxy, transducer hypothesis, and energy budget are stated.

## 3. Core Hypothesis

### 3.1 Central Question

Can specific biological observables be shown, under pre-registered protocols, to depend on a **named** environmental or laboratory field proxy at an effect size distinguishable from thermal and known backgrounds — **without violating known physics**?

### 3.2 Conceptual Proposition

GUH proposes that:
- Life and neural systems can be modeled as **nonlinear, noise-sensitive systems** (true, but not sufficient alone),
- GUH’s research bet is that **some** weak field proxy might matter — but the proxy must be **named** (e.g. geomagnetic field, ELF electromagnetic environment, radical-pair-relevant B-field, laboratory vacuum-noise proxy, or another explicitly chosen candidate),
- Interactions, if claimed, need a **transducer hypothesis**, an **energy budget versus \(kT\) and known noise**, and a **decisive null** — not appeals to unspecified “complex system behavior.”

This remains a **hypothesis**, pending operationalization and falsification.

### 3.3 What would falsify this (minimum)

- Failure to specify a field proxy **and** a transducer hypothesis within a stated revision cycle parks the claim as **non-operational**.
- A pre-registered protocol finds \(|\mathrm{effect}|\) below a stated \(\varepsilon\) at adequate power → that **proxy / transducer pair is abandoned** (not redefined via a new “complexity measure”).
- If the only viable models require couplings already excluded by fifth-force or equivalence-principle tests, the **cosmological-scalar route (Option B) is closed**.
- If claimed effect sizes cannot be reconciled with an energy budget versus \(kT\) and known backgrounds, the claim is rejected for that model class.
- If results cannot be independently reproduced under the same pre-registered conditions, the line of inquiry does not advance by narrative adjustment alone.

## 4. Methodological Framework

### 4.1 Research Paradigm (I.I.L.)

GUH is developed using the **Interference Intelligence Layer (I.I.L.)**, a framework for structured Human–AI collaborative reasoning.

Key characteristics:
- hypothesis-driven exploration,
- iterative refinement,
- separation of intuition from validation,
- full auditability of reasoning steps.

### 4.2 Claim grades (operational)

Every substantive statement in this program should be graded and kept auditable:

| Grade | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| Speculation | Heuristic; not yet model-shaped |
| Model | Named variables, assumptions, and energy budget |
| Prediction | Pre-registered, falsifiable observable |
| Confirmed | Independently replicated under stated protocol |

Human researchers retain **veto** over claim grade, publication language, and any applied framing. AI systems assist analysis; they do not authorize scientific status.

### 4.3 Role of AI

AI systems function as:
- analytical and synthesis tools,
- pattern exploration engines,
- hypothesis stress-testing agents.

AI systems do **not**:
- define truth,
- establish authority,
- make scientific claims.

All responsibility remains human.

## 5. Relationship to Existing Science

Compatibility with established physics is **not** a virtue signal for empty models. Any future quantitative GUH model **must** be checked against General Relativity, Quantum Field Theory, and existing fifth-force / equivalence-principle bounds. Compatibility is **earned by calculation**, not declared.

Neighbor literatures to engage (topic-level pointers, not claimed endorsements):
- **magnetoreception** and radical-pair mechanisms in biology,
- **stochastic resonance** and noise-assisted detection in nonlinear biological systems,
- laboratory tests of **light scalars** and **chameleon / screening** constraints,
- cosmological dark energy / the cosmological-constant problem as a **separate** open physics question (e.g. Weinberg’s framing of the CC problem) — not assumed as GUH’s coupling partner under Option A.

## 6. Limitations & Safeguards

This hypothesis explicitly avoids:
- metaphysical assertions,
- anthropocentric cosmology,
- deterministic interpretations,
- extrapolation into medicine or therapy,
- **disease claims** of any kind; applied briefs are **questions-only**,
- **metaphorical use of cosmology terms** in v1.2 working language (retired with the v1.1 Dark Energy branding).

Any future application claims would require:
- independent experimental validation,
- peer review,
- ethical and regulatory oversight.
